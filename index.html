Discover Mimice

Welcome to Mimice

Mimice is a small Dalmatian village made for a slower kind of holiday. Crystal-clear sea, the pebble beaches of Kutleša and Juto, and the shade of pine trees create that simple Mediterranean feeling that makes it easy to stay longer than you planned.

Wander the stone streets of the old village, discover hidden glimpses of the sea, and enjoy the peace of a place that has kept its authentic Dalmatian character.
