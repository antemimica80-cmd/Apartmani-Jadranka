Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, for what purposes, and what rights you have as a data subject under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What Data We Collect

This is a static website with no database of its own on a server. We do not collect or store personal data through the availability inquiry form. When you fill in the inquiry form and click "Send Inquiry," your browser opens a pre-filled message in your own email application — we only receive the details you enter (name, email, phone, number of guests, message) if and when you choose to actually send that email from your own account. The same applies to the "Phone," "WhatsApp," and "Email" buttons in the contact section — they simply open your own calling, WhatsApp, or email app, and we only receive your details if you choose to reach out.

Cookies and Local Storage

The website uses your browser's local storage solely to remember your chosen display language. This value stays only in your own browser, is never sent to us or any third party, and is not used for tracking. The site does not use cookies for analytics or advertising.

Third-Party Services

To display fonts and an interactive map, the site loads content from Google's servers (Google Fonts and Google Maps). When that content loads, your browser connects directly to Google, which may involve transmitting your IP address to Google independently of us. Google acts as a separate data controller for that processing — see Google's own Privacy Policy for details. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which as our hosting provider may log standard server access data (e.g., your IP address) under its own privacy policy.

Legal Basis and Purpose

When you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or email, we process your data based on your consent and/or to take steps prior to entering into an accommodation contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)), solely to respond to your inquiry and arrange a booking if applicable.

Data Retention

Since the website itself does not collect or store data, there is no site-level retention period. Data you send us directly (email, phone, WhatsApp message) is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for communication and any resulting booking, in line with normal business practice.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to request access to your personal data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object to processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Croatian Data Protection Agency (AZOP), www.azop.hr.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date of the last update is shown at the bottom of this page.
